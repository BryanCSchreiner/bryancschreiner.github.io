Bryan Schreiner

I build hands-on medical training: the models, the labs, and the lessons.

I've spent over a decade at Oregon Health & Science University, first as a facilities technician in the hospital, then running day-to-day operations in the VirtuOHSU Simulation Center, and later as a medical student, student leader, and research volunteer.

My work includes co-leading development of an ECMO training manikin, first-authoring a published simulation curriculum for orthopaedic residents, and teaching hands-on skills to learners of every level.
